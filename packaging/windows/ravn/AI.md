# Use of AI

AI was used: [Claude](https://claude.ai), by Anthropic, and [Grok](https://grok.com), by xAI. This file says where, and where it was not.

## The program

The Windows build workflow, the Ravn launcher (`launcher/ravn/`), `Ravn.exe`, the packaging script, the trophy port and these texts were written with Claude. The builds are made on GitHub Actions. The publisher tests each build on Windows before it is released.

Ravn follows the Kagekidan launcher of Sakura-Wars-PS4RT-Windows, which was written with Grok, and the trophy card comes from that port. Grok also created the `windows-1.2` tag and published the release.

The runtime is not an AI rewrite of valkyria-revolution-ps4rt. It is HekamiAeon's program, with its Windows build restored. The trophy card is the one from Sakura-Wars-PS4RT-Windows windows-1.1.3, carried over.

## Pictures, video and music

No image, video or music model was used.

- The logo was drawn by a program: **Ravn** in Playfair Display, a gold rule, a line-drawn feather, and **ᚼᚱᛅᚠᚾ**, set in Noto Sans Runic. The runes spell *hrafn*, Old Norse for raven, in Younger Futhark. They were checked letter by letter, not guessed.
- The moving background, feathers and snow on black, was rendered by a program.
- `theme.mp3` is an original melody, written for this launcher, in D Dorian. A program synthesizes a plucked string and a drone and plays it. Nobody was recorded. Nothing was sampled.

The programs are in `launcher/ravn/tools/` in the repository.

No SEGA illustration, logo, music or sound was used as a source, and none ships in this archive.

## What AI was not allowed to supply

- The game, or any instruction for obtaining it illegally.
- SEGA's key art, music or sounds.
- A claim that a feature works when it has not been tested on Windows.
